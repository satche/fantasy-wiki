---
title: Session Zero
date: 2026-01-01
description: Campaign's setting, lore and ruleset
---

A session zero is a pre-game meeting where the players and the <abbr title="Game Master">GM</abbr> align on different aspect of the campaign before starting it. It helps prevent mismatched expectations and sets the stage for smoother sessions.

A session zero would typically include the following.

- [ ] Game system, ruleset, homebrew
- [ ] Theme, lore, style of campaign
- [ ] Party composition, character relationships
- [ ] Safety tools, topics to avoid
- [ ] Frequency and durations of sessions
